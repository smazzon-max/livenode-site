Control an ATEM mixer from your Mac

Operate your ATEM video mixer from the same console where you run your other sources, meetings and audio.

Download LiveNode for Mac

One console for the whole show

In many live shows an ATEM mixer works alongside Zoom, OBS and extra cameras. LiveNode brings them together: you control the mixer without jumping between apps while you're on air.

Add cameras without cables

On top of the mixer's sources you can add one or more iPhones as cameras, connected with a QR code. Their video quality adapts to the network.

Your control room in your hand

With LiveNode Dock you use iPad and iPhone as a remote: follow the event up close and stay in control.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need another app to control the ATEM?

With LiveNode you control the mixer from the same console you use for your other sources.

Can I use an iPad as a remote?

Yes, with LiveNode Dock you use iPad and iPhone to run the control room from the venue floor.

How much does it cost?

The Mac app is free.
