Privacy policy
LiveNode Camera

Last updated: 11 November 2026. This policy covers the LiveNode Camera app for iPhone. For the website, your account and LiveNode Cloud, the general privacy policy applies.

1. Who we are

LiveNode Camera is an app by Production Management S.r.l. (Piemme). For any privacy question write to contact@livenode-software.com.

2. What the app does

The app turns your iPhone into a video camera for LiveNode, the Mac app used to produce live video. You connect by scanning the QR code shown on the Mac. The app sends video only: it does not capture or send audio.

3. Video

Video travels directly from the phone to the Mac you paired, over an encrypted connection (WebRTC, DTLS-SRTP). It does not pass through our servers, is not recorded or stored by the app and is not shared with third parties. If a direct connection is not possible, the app may use a relay server (TURN) that forwards already-encrypted data without being able to read it.

4. Connection messages

To let the phone and the Mac find each other, they exchange short technical messages through a real-time messaging service (Supabase). They contain: the random QR code, a random phone identifier, the phone and Mac names, the network information needed to connect (such as IP addresses) and the phone status (battery level, temperature, video quality). They contain no video. We do not keep them; the service provider may log technical connection data for operation and security under its own policy. To discover its public network address the app contacts Google STUN servers.

5. Data stored on the phone

The app stores on the phone only the pairing code, a random identifier and the Mac name, so it can reconnect without scanning again. Tap Disconnect to delete them.

6. Permissions

  • Camera: to send video to the Mac.
  • Local network: to connect to the Mac on the same network.

The app does not ask for access to the microphone. You can revoke permissions at any time in Settings.

7. What we do not do

No accounts, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party analytics, no sale of data.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children.

9. Your rights

Since we do not keep personal data that identifies you, there is no archive to access or delete. To exercise your GDPR rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection) write to contact@livenode-software.com.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be available in the app and on this page, with the date at the top updated.
