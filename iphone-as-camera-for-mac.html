Use your iPhone as a camera for live shows from your Mac

With LiveNode, the phone in your pocket becomes a camera for your control room: connect it with a QR code and control it from the Mac.

Download LiveNode for Mac

Why use your iPhone as a camera

A multi-camera live show doesn't always need more camcorders. An iPhone gives you an extra angle, fits anywhere and needs no cable back to the control room.

How to connect it

  1. Install LiveNode on the Mac and LiveNode Camera on the iPhone.
  2. In LiveNode choose “Add phone”: a QR code appears on the Mac.
  3. Scan the code with LiveNode Camera. The phone joins your sources with video and audio.

What you control from the console

Right-click the camera to adjust zoom, focus, torch and microphone, pick the lens and set the quality. You don't need to touch the phone during the show.

A weak network won't freeze the picture

When the connection gets worse, LiveNode lowers the stream quality instead of freezing the picture. Once the network is stable again, quality comes back up on its own. Video goes straight from the phone to the Mac, without passing through our servers.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a cable to connect the iPhone?

No. The iPhone connects to the Mac by scanning a QR code, and video travels over the network, straight from the phone to the Mac.

Can I use several iPhones at once?

Yes, every phone running LiveNode Camera becomes another source in the control room.

What if the Wi-Fi is weak?

LiveNode lowers the stream quality instead of freezing the picture, and raises it again when the network is stable.

How much does it cost?

The Mac app is free.
