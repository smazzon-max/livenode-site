How it works

From first launch to live in three steps.

  1. 01

    Install the control room

    Open the DMG file and drag LiveNode into your Applications folder. No account needed.

  2. 02

    Connect your sources

    Add cameras and windows. For the iPhone, open LiveNode Camera and scan the QR code: video goes straight to the Mac.

  3. 03

    Go live

    Set up the next shot in preview and press Take. If you like, record and save the show to the cloud.

Frequently asked questions

How much does LiveNode cost?

The Mac app is free. You only pay for LiveNode Cloud, the optional service that saves your live shows online, with a monthly subscription from €9 plus VAT.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 14.4 or later, with an Apple silicon or Intel processor.

How do I use my iPhone as a camera for a live show?

Install LiveNode Camera on your iPhone, choose “Add phone” in LiveNode and scan the QR code. Zoom, focus and torch are adjusted from the control room.

Can I control Zoom, OBS and ATEM?

Yes. From the same console you control Zoom meetings, switch scenes in OBS and operate an ATEM video mixer.

What happens if the network is weak?

LiveNode lowers the quality of the iPhone stream so you don't lose the picture, and raises it again as soon as the network is stable. You can also fix it manually with a right-click.

Does the video go through your servers?

No. Video goes straight from the phone to the Mac. Our service only helps the two devices find each other when they start.

Do I need an account?

Not to use the control room. You only need an account to subscribe to the Cloud and activate the plan on your Macs.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, anytime, from your Account page. The subscription stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for.
