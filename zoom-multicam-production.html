Multi-camera production for Zoom, from your Mac

A Zoom meeting or event with several shots, run from one console: cameras, iPhone, scenes and audio together.

Download LiveNode for Mac

More shots in a Zoom meeting

An online event looks more polished when the shot changes: the speaker, the room, the slides. With LiveNode you set up the next shot in preview and put it on air with Take.

Zoom and OBS from the same console

LiveNode controls Zoom meetings and switches OBS scenes without making you jump between windows. The audio mixer keeps per-channel levels under control.

How to set up your control room

  1. Install LiveNode on your Mac.
  2. Add your sources: cameras, windows and, if you like, your iPhone with a QR code.
  3. Connect Zoom and, if you use it, OBS.
  4. Set up the shot in preview and press Take.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use LiveNode with Zoom and OBS together?

Yes. From the same console you run Zoom meetings and switch scenes in OBS.

Can I add my iPhone as a camera?

Yes, with LiveNode Camera and a QR code.

Do I need an account?

No, the control room works without an account. You only need one for LiveNode Cloud.
