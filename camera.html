How it works

Connected in three steps.

  1. 01

    Install the app

    Install LiveNode Camera on your iPhone.

  2. 02

    Add the phone

    On the Mac, in LiveNode, choose “Add phone”: a QR code appears.

  3. 03

    Scan the code

    Scan it with LiveNode Camera. The phone joins your sources as a camera.

A camera you control from the Mac

You don't need to touch the phone during the show.

QR connection

No setup on the phone: scan the code and you're on.

Controlled from the Mac

Zoom, focus and torch, lens and quality, with a right-click on the camera.

Adaptive quality

When the network gets worse, the stream drops in quality instead of freezing, then comes back up.

Straight to the Mac

Video goes directly from the phone to the Mac. Our service only helps the two devices find each other.

Free: up to 2 iPhones at once. Cloud Start: 4. Cloud Pro and Studio: no limit. How many you can actually run depends on your Mac and network. Guide: iPhone as a camera for your Mac · Camera privacy
