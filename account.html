Two-step verification

When you sign in, besides your password we ask for a code from the authenticator app on your phone.

  1. Open an authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password.
  2. Scan the QR code. If you can't, enter the key below manually.
  3. Type the 6-digit code shown in the app.
