How it works

From first launch to live in three steps.

  1. 01

    Install the control room

    Open the DMG file and drag LiveNode into your Applications folder. No account needed.

  2. 02

    Connect your sources

    Add cameras, windows, images and video. For the iPhone, open LiveNode Camera and scan the QR code.

  3. 03

    Go live

    Set up the next shot in preview and press Take. Stream, record, and save the show to the cloud if you like.

Your whole control room in one window

Everything a live show needs, without switching between apps.

Sources and scenes

Windows, images, cameras, video and graphics, with transitions between scenes.

iPhone as a camera

Wireless video with a QR code. Zoom, focus and torch are adjusted from the control room.

Zoom, OBS and ATEM

Run meetings, switch scenes and operate the video mixer from the same console.

Clean output

A clean program feed to an ATEM mixer or over HDMI.

Streaming and recording

Stream over RTMP and record the show on your Mac.

Audio mixer

Per-channel levels and selectable outputs, always in view next to the pictures.
