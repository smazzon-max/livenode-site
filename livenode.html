How it works

From first launch to live in three steps.

  1. 01

    Install the control room

    Open the DMG file and drag LiveNode into your Applications folder. No account needed.

  2. 02

    Connect your sources

    Add cameras, windows, images and video. For the iPhone, open LiveNode Camera and scan the QR code.

  3. 03

    Go live

    Set up the next shot in preview and press Take. Stream, record, and save the show to the cloud if you like.
