How can we help?

Short guides to using LiveNode, from installation to your first live show.

Install LiveNode on your Mac

  1. Download the DMG file from the Download section.
  2. Open it and drag LiveNode into your Applications folder.
  3. Launch LiveNode. You don't need an account to use the control room.

You need a Mac with macOS 14.4 or later, with an Apple silicon or Intel processor.

Connect your iPhone as a camera

  1. Install LiveNode Camera on your iPhone.
  2. On the Mac, in LiveNode, choose “Add phone”: a QR code appears.
  3. Scan the code with LiveNode Camera. The phone joins your sources as a camera.

Zoom, focus and torch are adjusted from the control room, with a right-click on the camera.

If the phone video slows down

  1. Leave quality on Auto: LiveNode lowers it when the network gets worse and raises it again on its own.
  2. If you prefer a fixed value, right-click the camera and choose the quality.
  3. When you can, put the Mac and the phone on the same stable, uncrowded Wi-Fi network.

Video goes straight from the phone to the Mac: it doesn't pass through our servers.

Use Zoom, OBS and ATEM

  1. Add the app or mixer you want to control.
  2. From then on you control it from the LiveNode console.
  3. To stay on the venue floor, use iPad or iPhone as a remote with LiveNode Dock.

Run Zoom meetings, switch OBS scenes and operate an ATEM video mixer without changing window.

Link a Mac to your account

  1. Open LiveNode on your Mac and go to Settings › About.
  2. Click “Sign in”: the website opens with a code.
  3. Check that the code matches the one in the app and click “Link”.

You can link up to 3 Macs. See and remove them in the Devices section of your account.

Upload and share recordings

  1. With an active LiveNode Cloud plan, in the app go to Settings › Recording › Cloud recordings.
  2. Uploaded recordings appear in your account.
  3. Click “Share” and choose how long the link lasts: 7 days, 30 days or no expiry. You can revoke it anytime.

Anyone with the link can watch and download the recording without an account.

Change plan or cancel

  1. Open your account, Subscription and billing section.
  2. Click “Manage billing”: Stripe's secure page opens.
  3. From there you can change card, download invoices or cancel.

After cancelling, your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

Keep your account safe

  1. Open your account, Profile and security section.
  2. Turn on two-step verification and scan the QR code with an authenticator app.
  3. If you used your account on a computer that isn't yours, click “Sign out everywhere” in Devices.

With two-step verification on, we'll also ask for the 6-digit code from your phone when you sign in.

Frequently asked questions

How much does LiveNode cost?

The Mac app is free. You only pay for LiveNode Cloud, the optional service that saves your live shows online, with a monthly subscription.

Do I need an account to use the control room?

No. You only need an account for LiveNode Cloud.

Does the video go through your servers?

No. Video goes straight from the phone to the Mac. Our service only helps the two devices find each other when they start.

How many Macs can I link?

Up to 3 Macs per account.
