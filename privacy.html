1. Data controller

Production Management S.r.l. (Piemme), registered office at Via Noce 11, 38121 Trento (TN), Italy, VAT no. IT10395850968. Privacy contact: marco@piol.it.

2. What data we process

  • Account: email address and password (stored only in encrypted form, which we cannot read).
  • Linked Macs: computer name, link date and last use, app version.
  • Subscription: plan, status, renewal dates and Stripe customer ID. Payment data (card, billing address, VAT number) is collected by Stripe.
  • LiveNode Cloud: the recordings you upload, with title, length, size and date, and the share links you create. Recordings are visible only to you and to people you send a link to; links can expire and can be revoked.
  • Technical server data: IP address and security logs, for as long as strictly necessary.

We don't use profiling cookies or advertising analytics. The website only stores the technical data of your sign-in session in your browser, and your language choice.

3. Why, and on what legal basis

  • Creating and managing the account, linking Macs, providing the Cloud: performance of a contract.
  • Invoicing and tax obligations: legal obligation.
  • Security and abuse prevention: legitimate interest.

4. Who we share it with

  • Supabase (database, authentication): [project region, e.g. EU].
  • Stripe (payments): independent controller for payment data.
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting) and, for the Cloud, Supabase Storage for storing recordings (same region as the Supabase project).
  • Email delivery service for sign-up confirmations [e.g. Resend].

These providers act as processors or independent controllers. If a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, it does so with the safeguards required by the GDPR (e.g. standard contractual clauses).

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and related data: as long as the account exists; deleted when you delete it.
  • Invoices and tax data: 10 years, as required by law (kept on Stripe and in our accounting records).
  • Cloud recordings: as long as they are in your storage; after the subscription ends [retention period, e.g. 30 days].

6. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, and object to processing, by writing to marco@piol.it. You can delete your account yourself from the Account page. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it) or with the authority of your country.

7. Children

The service is not intended for people under 16.

8. Updates

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top and, for important changes, we let you know.
